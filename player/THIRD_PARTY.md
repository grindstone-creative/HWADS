# Third-party software

`browser/vendor/qrcodegen.js` contains Project Nayuki’s QR Code Generator, compiled from TypeScript. Its complete MIT license is preserved in the file.

Author: Project Nayuki. Source: https://www.nayuki.io/page/qr-code-generator-library and https://github.com/nayuki/QR-Code-generator. The same bundled encoder is used by the HWADS plugin; no CDN or QR service is contacted.
